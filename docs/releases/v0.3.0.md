# Cats Bot 0.3.0 — Standard Preview

The first release under the new identity (`cats-inc/cats-bot`, data in
`~/.catsbot`). One hub now runs up to three bots, each in a process of its
own, and one bot can coordinate the others.

## There is no upgrade from 0.2.x

0.3.0 is a clean break before public launch: it reads none of the 0.2.x
locations, names or releases (SPEC-007).

- Do not use **Check for Updates** in 0.2.x. Its feed can follow GitHub's
  rename redirect to 0.3.0 and install it over 0.2.x.
- In 0.2.x, turn off **Start at login** first. Its login entry is named after
  the old AUMID and would start 0.3.0 against an empty root.
- Uninstall 0.2.x (its uninstall entry has a different GUID), then install
  0.3.0.
- Before installing 0.3.0 you may delete the 0.2.x leftovers by hand:
  - `~/.cats/bots`
  - `%APPDATA%\Cats Bot`
  - `%LOCALAPPDATA%\@cats-inccats-bots-updater`

  After installing, `%APPDATA%\Cats Bot` is the hub's own user data.
- Tokens stored by 0.2.x, such as a Telegram token, are not carried over.

## One hub, several bots

- **One hub.** Cats Bot is one hub per account and installation, with one
  tray icon, one login item and one update coordinator.
- **A process per bot.** Each bot runs as a core process of its own, with its
  own Runtime and provider CLI.
  - A crash of the hub leaves the bots running, and the next hub reattaches.
  - A core that ends unasked is restarted, at most three times in ten minutes.
- **Settings → Bots** lists every bot with its state:
  - It creates, starts, stops, orders and sets start automatically.
  - It archives, restores and deletes bots. Deleting asks twice and only
    takes archived bots.
  - At most three bots run; archived bots do not count.
- **The tray** keeps the familiar menu with one bot. With more, each bot gets
  a submenu, and the tooltip sums what waits for you.
- **Bot windows.** Each bot's notes, floating Cat and settings window belong
  to it, carry its name and group separately in the Windows taskbar.
- **Desktop control.**
  - One bot at a time uses the desktop and the clipboard.
  - Every bot excludes the hub and its own windows from control.
- **Conversations.** Conversation windows use the same message components and
  styles as Cats Desktop. Text, tool and status segments keep their order
  through streaming, completion, cancellation and reopening. Existing
  transcripts are unchanged.

## The coordinator

The first bot holds the coordinator role. You can give it to another bot, or
clear it, in Settings → Bots or with `cats-bot hub coordinator`.

- **What the holder can do.** It lists the other bots, hands them work and
  receives their answers in the conversation that asked. It can also start,
  stop and pause the heartbeat of other bots.
- **Where its messages arrive.** Another bot receives the holder's messages in
  a conversation of their own, "Messages from" the holder, apart from your
  direct message with that bot. They are not mirrored to Telegram.
- **What waits for you.** Creating a bot, starting a stopped bot, and passing
  the role on each wait for your confirmation.
  - A request expires after 10 minutes, or when the role moves.
  - The holder has one request before you at a time. One you declined is not
    asked again for a minute.
  - A bot that keeps failing is yours to start again, not the holder's.
- **What it can never do.**
  - answer requests meant for you
  - change another bot's settings, permissions or secrets
  - read another bot's conversations
  - archive or delete bots
- **Limits.** By default a chain of messages has at most 4 hops, each message
  and each reply counting as one, and the holder sends at most 10 messages an
  hour. Both can be changed in Settings → Bots.
- **What this is not.** These are limits on the coordinator's tools, not a
  security boundary: a native provider CLI runs under your account with its
  own access.

## Compatibility, upgrade and recovery

- **A new minor.** 0.3 breaks 0.2's contracts:
  - The data root is now `~/.catsbot` (`CATS_BOT_HOME`); `CATS_BOTS_HOME` is
    gone.
  - The application id is `io.catsinc.catsbot`.
  - The CLI is `cats-bot`.
  - New hub files: `bots.json`, `hub-state.json`, the hub lock and control
    descriptor, `coordinator/` and `logs/hub-audit.jsonl`.
  - Profiles record `environment: { kind: 'native' }`; anything else is
    refused.
- **Upgrade.** There is no 0.2-to-0.3 upgrade and no data migration (above).
  Within 0.3, files are read field by field; a corrupt registry or profile is
  kept as it is and reported.
- **Recovery.** A delete that stopped partway finishes at the next start, and
  the registry follows the bot folders. To roll back, quit Cats Bot, back up
  `~/.catsbot`, and reinstall the older 0.3 build.
